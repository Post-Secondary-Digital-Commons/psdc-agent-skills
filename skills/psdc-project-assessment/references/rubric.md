# PSDC Assessment Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 4. Scores measure available evidence, not effort.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Missing or contradicted |
| 1 | Proposed; scope or owner incomplete |
| 2 | Documented coherently at a named revision |
| 3 | Contracted and structurally validated with positive and negative evidence |
| 4 | Implemented and behaviorally verified; deployment claims still need separate evidence |

## Dimensions

1. Authority and decision traceability
2. Domain vocabulary and bounded contexts
3. Cross-repository dependencies and interfaces
4. Contract completeness and compatibility
5. Identity, authorization, privacy, and consent
6. Failure, retry, revocation, recovery, and idempotency
7. Federation and institution sovereignty
8. Licensing, provenance, and upstream maintenance
9. Test quality and independent negative evidence
10. Operational readiness and observability
11. Documentation quality, links, stubs, and semantic uniqueness
12. Implementation handoff quality and vertical-slice feasibility

For each dimension report: score, evidence, counter-evidence, uncertainty, highest
risk, and the smallest artifact or test that would increase confidence.
